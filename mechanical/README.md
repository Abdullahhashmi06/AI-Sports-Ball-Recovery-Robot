# mechanical

Mechanical design: CAD models, 3D-print files, chassis, ball intake, storage bin, and dispensing mechanism.

## Status

The [V4 suction and automatic-dispensing proposal](suction-v4/README.md) includes
Fusion-compatible CAD, source, renders, and geometry checks. It is a review draft,
not fabricated hardware or a validated replacement for the agreed architecture.

Automatic one-ball dispensing is a required feature in this proposal. See the
[dispensing sequence and bench tests](suction-v4/AUTOMATIC_DISPENSING.md) and the
[materials and parts specification](../hardware/suction-v4/MATERIALS_AND_COMPONENTS.md).
